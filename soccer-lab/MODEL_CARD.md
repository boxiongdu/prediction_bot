# EPL pre-match research baseline

## Scope

Predict regulation-time home win / draw / away win for EPL fixtures two hours before kickoff. Championship results inform team state, including teams subsequently promoted. This package produces research forecasts, not trading instructions. It does not connect to Kalshi or place orders. No bookmaker odds, news sentiment, or LLM probability adjustments enter these models. Neither model is represented as a replication of a cited paper.

## Candidates fixed before validation

- **Plain Elo control:** Initial EPL rating 1500, Championship rating 1350; home advantage 60 rating points; update K = 20 or 40. A Davidson mapping produces all three outcomes, with equal-strength draw rate .24 or .30 for prediction. Rating updates consistently use the .26 draw-rate mapping and expected score `P(home win) + .5 P(draw)`. No goal margin enters Elo updates. Existing teams keep their ratings across seasons and divisions.
- **Four-feature attack/defense model:** Home attack, home defense, away attack, away defense are log goal rates relative to 1.4 goals, with eight prior games of shrinkage. Goal sums and effective sample counts decay by .90 or .97 per completed match. Defense is sign-reversed so larger values mean fewer goals conceded. A standardized multinomial logistic regression uses C = .1 or 1.0. Home and away columns allow separate learned coefficients. There are no hand-selected post-validation feature changes or fitted opponent-strength corrections.
- **Historical frequency benchmark:** Fixed EPL home/draw/away proportions from 2023–24. This benchmark is reported separately; candidate selection chooses the lowest validation log loss among the eight Elo/logistic candidates.

These summaries use scored and conceded goals only. Historical shots are ingested for auditing and possible future experiments but not used by V1. The four-feature compression may lose useful information; improvement over Elo must be demonstrated, not assumed.

## Championship and promotion assumptions

A team first encountered in Championship receives the 1350 rating prior. Ratings carry forward without an automatic promotion boost. Observed goal-rate history is translated on transitions: Championship to EPL multiplies past scored-goal sums by .85 and divides conceded-goal sums by .85; relegation applies the inverse. Effective sample count is unchanged, and shrinkage is applied afterward. An upcoming fixture's observed division triggers the translation without consulting future fixtures. Teams with no history receive neutral goal-rate priors.

The 150-point initial division gap and .85 translation are predeclared assumptions, not estimates validated from enough historical promotions. Pools lacking cross-division games cannot identify their relative Elo level from match results alone. League One history is absent, so newly encountered Championship teams receive priors. Squad turnover and management changes are not explicitly modeled.

## Time and leakage controls

Every historical feature row is generated by replaying source results in `available_utc` order and consuming only results available at or before that fixture's `forecast_utc`. A match's own final statistics cannot update its pre-match row. Input order is normalized deterministically. No full-season averages, future division membership, or future results initialize a past team's state.

The ingestion layer assumes English fixture times are Europe/London and sets historical result availability to kickoff + 24 hours. This is a conservative simulation proxy, not verified historical publication timestamps. The backtest therefore cannot establish that the provider actually delivered every record by its simulated cutoff. Raw historical files may also include later corrections. Real deployment requires timestamped snapshots and observed receipt times.

## Evaluation and updates

- 2022–23: state warmup only; no classifier training labels.
- 2023–24: classifier fitting.
- 2024–25: compare the eight predeclared candidates; choose minimum log loss, deterministic ID tie-break. This is development performance subject to selection optimism.
- 2025–26: optional single held-out evaluation. A separate explicit call requires an existing `selection.json`. The chosen configuration is reused without selection or tuning; classifier coefficients are refit on 2023–24 and 2024–25 only. Coefficients remain frozen for the held-out season, while ratings and goal summaries update with eligible earlier results.
- 2026–27 forecasts: retain the selected configuration, fit classifier coefficients using completed earlier modeling seasons, and update team summaries with available current-season results. This is an expanding **seasonal refit** procedure, not per-match classifier retraining.

The selection includes development-data and model-code SHA256 hashes. A changed development dataset or model implementation requires a new experiment directory. Existing holdout artifacts cannot be overwritten by repeated test calls. This is a guardrail, not a security boundary: humans must not inspect held-out results, change the model, and describe a later evaluation of that same season as untouched. Any changes informed by holdout findings require new future evaluation data.

## Metrics and artifacts

Primary: multiclass log loss and multiclass Brier score (sum over three classes, **not** divided by three). Secondary: accuracy and ten-bin one-versus-rest calibration tables for H/D/A. Calibration tables are diagnostic, not post-hoc recalibration. Selection is exclusively by validation log loss.

`selection.json` freezes parameters; `validation.csv` contains candidate forecasts; `metrics.json` contains development metrics; optional `test.csv` and `test_metrics.json` contain the held-out evaluation. Every prediction records the cutoff and last consumed result's availability. Data lineage and decision memos are handled by the surrounding orchestrator.

Uncertainty intervals, comparison against executable market prices, trading fees, portfolio risk, drawdown, and evidence of profitability are outside this phase. Low forecast loss does not by itself establish a trading edge.
